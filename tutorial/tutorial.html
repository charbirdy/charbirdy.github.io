How to Embed A Service-Hosted Video Into A Website

In this very simple tutorial, you'll be shown how to insert a video into your website. For this example, a youtube video will be used. Look for the yellow box to navigate where to going.

  1. Get your video ready and look for the "share" option right under the video. This will include the embed code you'll need for your website
  2. Look for an "embed" button, which should be the first, and click on that
  3. Once clicked, a window will pop up. To the right is the embed code. On the bottom right is the "copy" button. Clicking that will be copied to your clipboard.
  4. Once copied, open up your website editor and simply paste the link. Then, click "commit changes"
  5. Wait for your website to process the changes. After that, your video should be in your designated website.

And that's it! Enjoy some cute guinea pigs.
