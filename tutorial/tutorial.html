How to Embed A Service-Hosted Video Into A Website

In this very simple tutorial, you'll be shown how to insert a video into your website. For this example, a youtube video will be used and GitHub will be the website editor. Any other service will work the same. Note that some creators won't allow their video to be used outside that service, so this won't work for every video. Look for the yellow box to navigate where to go.

  1. Get your video ready and look for the "share" option right under the video. This will include the embed code you'll need for your website.
  2. Look for the "embed" button, which should be the first, and click on that.
  3. Once clicked, a window will pop up. To the right is the embed code. On the bottom right is the "copy" button. Click it so that it'll be copied to your clipboard. The video contents will always be in an "<iframe>" tag.
  4. Once copied, open up your website editor and simply paste the link. Then, click "commit changes" or whatever button confirms your edits. Your website will update to its current state.
  5. Wait for your website to process the changes. After that, your video should be in your designated website.

And that's it! Enjoy some cute guinea pigs.
