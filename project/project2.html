How to Embed a Service-Hosted Video Into a Website

Main Page Page 1 Page 3 Page 4 Page 5

In this simple tutorial you'll learn how to insert a video (e.g., YouTube) into your website using the service's embed code.

  1. Open the video you want to embed and click the "Share" button beneath the video.
  2. Choose "Embed" from the sharing options and copy the provided HTML iframe code.
  3. Paste the iframe snippet into your website editor where you want the video to appear.
  4. Save or commit your changes and publish the site if required by your hosting service.
  5. Wait for the hosting service to process the update, then verify the video appears on the page.
