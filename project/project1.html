Meet the Members

A small team of friendly sports-loving characters

Seally

Seally is a seal who loves to play sports. She is excited to be a part of this team alongside her coach. She likes to swim and catch fish, and she’s the best at swimming. She enjoys cool places and is always encouraging teammates.

Bobby

Bobby is a lobster who is always ready to play. With his sharp claws, he can be competitive and strong. He likes to play beachball with his sportsmates and loves to relax on the shore between matches.

Cappy

Cappy is a capybara who is fast on his feet and uses clever tactics. He enjoys relaxing in saunas during breaks and often shares treats with teammates. His calm style keeps the team steady.

Octavia

Octavia is an octopus who has a big advantage with her tentacles — she can multitask to get things done quickly. She brings energy to practice and loves teasing teammates in a friendly way.
